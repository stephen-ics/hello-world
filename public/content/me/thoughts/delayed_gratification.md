# Delayed Gratification

*July 2025*

Delayed gratification has always been a concept that has greatly interested me because to me it is sacrificing a part of your life for a different part. Or more simply put, the transaction of time.

I think most people would agree that they would be willing to trade 24 hours of their life for a lifetime of freedom. But I also feel like most people would agree that ones childhood is important, and it's not worth studying every second of your childhood in order to become wildly successful in the future.

So then what ratio of your current time vs future time is worth the trade? It's certainly 1 to 1, and if it's not worth studying every second of your childhood its not even 1 to 5.

We also need to take into consideration that some people may believe that not all years are worth the same, you probably don't remember what you did when you were 2 and you may not be able to do as many activities past your physical prime, we should factor this in when trading our time... Right?

I hope you get my dilemma, I'm not smart enough to calculate the answer to that in order to "maximize" ones happiness throughout their life, nor do I think it's possible or that we even should.

My childhood is a part of my life that I deeply treasure, there is nothing that could make me trade it away. And me personally, I n't believe in delayed gratification either. **You have to enjoy the journey**.
